Release candidate for milestone 9: the whole game, for the customer's
manual playthrough. Not a final release.

- The Colonel's Bequest plays from the start to the ending, in HD (vector
  backgrounds at any whole-number scale) or original mode.
- Parser, text windows, menus and the mouse cursor as in the original.
- Music and sound on AdLib (Nuked OPL3) or the Roland MT-32 (mt32emu; bring
  your own ROMs, see README.md).
- The game's own File menu saves and restores; saves go to `saves/` next
  to the configuration.

Binaries for Linux (glibc 2.38 or newer) and Windows, x64 and ARM64.
Bring your own copy of the game; see README.md in the archive.
